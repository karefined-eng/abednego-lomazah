Why Students Trust Their University Doctor

Navigating the academic and administrative landscape at the University of Ghana requires dedicated mentorship, structured preparation, and proactive advocacy. From organizing intensive Level 100 organic chemistry and African studies tutorials to compiling past questions (PASSCO) across five colleges, Abednego Lomazah combines clinical discipline with student-first service. His ongoing efforts bridge the gap between student leadership and real-world academic impact.
