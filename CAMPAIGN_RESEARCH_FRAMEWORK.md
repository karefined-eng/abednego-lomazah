# Campaign Website UI/UX Comparative Research Framework

## 1. Research Objectives
- Identify the UI/UX best practices of major political campaign websites worldwide.
- Identify the UI/UX best practices of successful student union/university election campaign websites.
- Compare these findings against the current Abednego Lomazah site to identify gaps and areas for improvement.
- Synthesize findings into a prioritized redesign brief.

## 2. Evaluation Criteria
For each site researched, we will evaluate:
- **Visual Identity:** Color palette, typography, imagery style, overall "vibe" (e.g., authoritative, grassroots, modern).
- **First-Visit Flow (Hero Section):** What is the immediate impression? How is video/motion used? What is the primary Call to Action (CTA)?
- **Storytelling & Narrative:** How is the candidate's story told? Is it chronological, issue-based, or personal? How is text balanced with media?
- **Video & Media Integration:** How are campaign videos, rallies, and social media (TikTok/Instagram) embedded? Are they auto-playing, modal, or inline?
- **Navigation & Architecture:** How easy is it to find the platform, volunteer options, or updates?
- **Responsive & Mobile UX:** How do the layout and interactions (like carousels or media rails) adapt to mobile screens?
- **Calls to Action (CTAs):** What actions are users driven to take (e.g., donate, volunteer, vote, join WhatsApp)?

## 3. Target Categories
- **Tier 1: Major National Political Campaigns (Global)**
  - US Presidential Campaigns (e.g., recent prominent examples).
  - European/UK National Campaigns.
  - Prominent African National Campaigns (if high-quality digital examples are available).
- **Tier 2: High-Profile Student Union/University Campaigns**
  - Major UK Student Union (NUS) campaigns.
  - US University Student Body President campaigns.
  - Other notable university campaigns with strong digital presence.

## 4. Output Deliverables
- A comprehensive markdown report (`CAMPAIGN_UX_COMPARISON.md`) detailing the findings.
- A prioritized redesign brief (`REDESIGN_BRIEF.md`) outlining specific, actionable changes for the Abednego Lomazah site.
