Advocacy Track Record: Delivered Petitions & Official Interventions

True student leadership is measured by verifiable actions and relentless advocacy. Over the past three years, Abednego Lomazah and his dedicated team have drafted, formally signed, and hand-delivered over 13 official letters and petitions to university executives, administrative directors, and campus stakeholders. These interventions address critical student challenges across the University of Ghana, ranging from academic grading anomalies and shuttle transportation deficits to campus Wi-Fi infrastructure and fee payment deadlines.
