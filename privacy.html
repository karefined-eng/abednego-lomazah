PRIVACY & LEGAL DISCLOSURE

Transparency, independent supporter operation, and data privacy.

1. Independent Supporter Initiative & Legal Disclaimer

This website (abednego-lomazah-site.vercel.app) is an independent, student-curated academic resource and advocacy archive. It was conceptualized, built, and maintained by student supporters and peer tutors dedicated to advancing the academic welfare and leadership vision of Abednego Lomazah at the University of Ghana.

Institutional Disclaimer: This website is NOT an official administrative domain of the University of Ghana, nor is it operated by or affiliated with the University of Ghana Students' Representative Council (UGSRC), the University of Ghana Computing Systems (UGCS), or Abednego Lomazah directly. All study packs, past questions (PASSCO), tutorial announcements, and petition records presented here are compiled and verified by independent student volunteers for educational and informational purposes only.

Content Ownership & Public Mirroring Disclosure: We do not own or claim copyright over any of the academic resources, petition letters, tutorial slides, or public images displayed on this platform. All original content, compiled study packs, PASSCO collections, and advocacy documents belong entirely to Abednego Lomazah and his tutorial teams. As an independent supporter network, our sole function is to mirror and index these publicly available assets—including Google Drive links from his public channels and our public WhatsApp groups within the university community—to expand their reach and make them easily discoverable via public search engines for the benefit of all University of Ghana students.

2. Takedown Requests & Copyright Respect

If any lecturer, rights holder, or content owner believes any material listed on this website exceeds the intended scope of sharing or infringes their rights, please submit a request through our dedicated removal request page. We will promptly review the notice and remove or disable access to the specific material in question upon verification.

This website does not claim or assume any license from academic staff, the University of Ghana, or external content providers. We are a student volunteer initiative that provides access only to materials that have been made publicly available through Abednego Lomazah's channels and our university community sharing network.

3. Anonymous Analytics & Data Collection

We respect student privacy and practice data minimization. This website does not collect, store, or process any personally identifiable information (PII), student ID numbers, academic transcripts, or login credentials.

To monitor site performance and optimize loading speeds for students accessing resources across campus Wi-Fi or mobile data networks, we utilize anonymous analytics services:

  • Vercel Web Analytics & Speed Insights: Collects anonymized, non-intrusive metrics such as page views, referring domains, and Core Web Vitals without using tracking cookies or collecting IP addresses.
  • Google Analytics (GA4): Utilized to understand general visitor traffic patterns and academic resource popularity to help us prioritize which course study packs to compile next.
