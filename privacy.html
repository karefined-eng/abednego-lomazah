Privacy & legal disclosure

Transparency, collaboration, and data privacy.

1. About this website and collaboration

This website (abednego-lomazah-site.vercel.app) is maintained in collaboration with the official publicity team supporting Abednego Lomazah. It brings together his public updates, academic resources, campaign information, and the #EVERLASTING message wall for the University of Ghana community.

The website is a public communications and resource platform for Abednego Lomazah. It is not a University of Ghana administrative service and should not replace official notices issued through the University's own channels. Information presented here is published for community access, awareness, and engagement in coordination with the publicity work for Abednego Lomazah.

Content ownership: Materials, statements, images, study resources, campaign records, and other original content supplied by Abednego Lomazah or his publicity team remain the property of their respective rights holders. The website presents those materials for the purposes of public communication and resource access. Do not reproduce or redistribute protected material beyond the permission granted by its owner.

2. Takedown requests & copyright respect

If any lecturer, rights holder, content owner, or member of the publicity team believes material listed on this website exceeds the intended scope of sharing or infringes their rights, please submit a request through our dedicated removal request page. We will review the notice and remove or disable access to the specific material when appropriate and after verification.

Content remains subject to the rights and permissions of its owners. Requests concerning Abednego Lomazah's publicity materials may also be raised through the official publicity channels linked on this website.

3. Information collected through the website

We collect only the information needed to operate the website and moderate the #EVERLASTING message wall. When a visitor submits a message, the website collects the name or initials entered by the visitor and the message text. The submission is stored securely in the site's moderation database, reviewed before publication, and may be displayed publicly if approved. Rejected submissions may be retained privately for moderation and safety records.

Please do not include student ID numbers, academic records, passwords, financial information, health information, or other sensitive personal information in a message. Visitors should submit only content they are comfortable having reviewed by the publicity team and potentially published on the public wall.

The website also uses analytics and performance services to understand general traffic, improve reliability, and monitor page performance. These services may process technical or usage information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Vercel Web Analytics & Speed Insights: Used for site performance and visitor-traffic insights.
  • Google Analytics (GA4): Used to understand general page-usage patterns and resource interest.
