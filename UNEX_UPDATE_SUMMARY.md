# Campaign Update Summary: UNEX GH Reports

## The Official X/Social Account
The relevant account reporting on the University of Ghana SRC elections is **University Nexus (UNEX GH)**, operating as `@unexgh_official` on X (Twitter) and Instagram, and `unexghofficial` on Facebook. It is a student-driven platform covering campus news and elections under hashtags like `#UGDecides26` and `#Election360`.

## The Disqualification Report
On August 22, 2026, UNEX GH broke the following update across its platforms:
> "Abednego Lomazah–Samuel Espan Bissah ticket disqualified from contesting the 2026 UGSRC Elections over CGPA requirements. The Electoral Commission says academic verification found that Abednego Lomazah did not meet the required CGPA threshold."

## Alignment with Campaign Materials
This public reporting perfectly aligns with the two images provided for the campaign website:
1. **The Official Communiqué (Team Abednego):** Acknowledges the end of the 69th SRC race, stating that while the MIS showed he met the requirements at the time of filing, later consideration of unavailable results determined he did not meet the Medical School's academic standing.
2. **The Eligibility Explainer (The Abednego Eligibility Issue):** Explains *why* this happened—a delay in fee payment caused a delay in course registration, meaning his Level 300 results were not reflected on his MIS portal (which showed a 3.06 GPA) when he filed. The Electoral Commission later used internally held results to disqualify him, allegedly introducing a new clause to do so.

## Recommendation for the Website
The public narrative (driven by UNEX GH) simply states he failed to meet the CGPA requirement. The campaign website must immediately counter this with the context provided in the two uploaded images. 

We should publish a major update on the Homepage and Campaign Story page titled **"The Eligibility Issue: What Voters Should Know,"** featuring the official communiqué and the detailed explanation of the MIS discrepancy. This ensures the campaign controls the narrative rather than letting the UNEX GH headline stand unchallenged.
