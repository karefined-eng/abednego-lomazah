# Mobile Navigation Patterns for Student Election Campaigns

Based on an analysis of high-performing student union election portals (e.g., UNC Board of Elections, York SU) and campaign strategy guides (Canva, College Raptor), here is how top student campaigns structure their mobile navigation.

## 1. The Header (Top Bar) Structure
Top student campaigns treat the mobile header as a branding anchor and a quick-escape hatch, not a filing cabinet.

- **The Logo/Name:** Always positioned on the top left. In student campaigns, name recognition is paramount. The candidate's name (e.g., "ABEDNEGO LOMAZAH") acts as the home button.
- **The Hamburger Menu (☰):** Almost universally placed on the top right. It must be large enough for easy tapping (minimum 44x44px touch target).
- **The Persistent Action (Optional but powerful):** Some highly optimized campaigns place a small, persistent "Donate" or "Vote" button directly in the header next to the hamburger menu, so the primary action is never hidden behind a tap.

## 2. The Menu Interaction (When Opened)
When the hamburger menu is tapped, the interaction must be decisive.

- **Full-Screen vs. Dropdown:** The best student sites use a full-screen or massive dropdown overlay that covers the page content. This forces focus on navigation and prevents accidental scrolling of the page beneath.
- **Visual Hierarchy within the Menu:**
  - **Primary Links (Massive font):** Home, Platform/Issues, Story.
  - **Secondary Links (Smaller font):** Resources, News, Contact.
  - **The CTA (Button style):** The primary call to action (e.g., "Join WhatsApp", "Donate", "Volunteer") is styled as a distinct button *inside* the menu, usually at the bottom or top of the list, contrasting sharply with the text links.

## 3. Emphasizing the Primary Action
Student elections are won on momentum and direct contact (often via WhatsApp or Discord).

- **The "Sticky" Bottom Bar:** A rising trend in mobile campaign UX is a persistent sticky bar at the bottom of the screen (similar to an app tab bar) with a single, high-contrast button like "Join the Campaign" or "Vote Now." This ensures the CTA is always within thumb's reach, regardless of how far the user scrolls.
- **In-Menu Emphasis:** If a sticky bar isn't used, the CTA inside the hamburger menu must use the campaign's brightest accent color (e.g., Gold or Red) to draw the eye immediately upon opening.

## 4. What to Avoid (Common Mistakes)
- **Nested Menus (Dropdowns within Dropdowns):** Student campaigns don't have enough content to justify nested menus. Keep the architecture flat.
- **Hidden Social Links:** Don't bury Instagram or TikTok links at the bottom of the footer. They should be prominent in the mobile menu overlay, as social proof is critical for youth voters.
- **"Mystery Meat" Navigation:** Avoid clever or ambiguous labels. Use clear terms like "Campaign Story," "Resources," and "Platform."

## 5. Application to Abednego Lomazah's Site
To align the Abednego Lomazah site with these top-tier mobile UX patterns:

1. **Keep the Header Clean:** Maintain the left-aligned name and right-aligned hamburger menu. Ensure the touch target for the menu toggle is large and responsive.
2. **Enhance the Menu Overlay:** When the menu opens, it should feel like a dedicated navigation screen. The links (HOME, RESOURCES, UPDATES) should be large and legible.
3. **Add Socials to the Menu:** Move or duplicate the Instagram/TikTok/LinkedIn links into the mobile menu overlay so they are easily accessible without scrolling to the footer.
4. **Consider a Sticky CTA:** If the primary goal is WhatsApp channel growth or TikTok follows, consider adding a persistent "Join WhatsApp" button either in the header or as a sticky element on mobile.
