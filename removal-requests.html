How to Request Removal of Content

This page is provided for lecturers, rights holders, or content owners who believe material listed on this website should be removed or disabled because it exceeds the intended scope of sharing or infringes intellectual property rights.

What to include in your request

  • Your name and role: e.g. course instructor, content owner, or authorized representative.
  • The URL of the material: the specific page or link on this site where the content appears.
  • Description of the material: explain why the content should be removed or restricted.
  • Proof of ownership or authority: if available, attach or describe evidence showing you are authorized to request removal.

Review process

Upon receipt of a valid request, we will review the notice as quickly as possible. If the request is verified, we will remove or disable access to the specific content in question.

Important notes

This website does not claim any license from academic staff, the University of Ghana, or external content providers. We only publish material that has been shared publicly through Abednego Lomazah’s channels or by supporting student groups, and removal requests are handled in good faith.
