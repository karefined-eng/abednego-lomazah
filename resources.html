About the Student Course Library & Academic Support

Welcome to the student-curated University of Ghana Course Library (unofficial), curated and maintained by Abednego Lomazah as part of his commitment to proactive student support and academic excellence. Whether you are a first-year student navigating Level 100 introductory modules or an advanced undergraduate preparing for rigorous departmental examinations, this compiled repository provides instant access to verified lecture slides, past questions (PASSCO), revision tutorials, and study guides.

How This Compiled Library Supports Your Studies

Academic success at the University of Ghana depends heavily on timely access to structured learning materials. Through continuous collaboration with class presidents, academic officers, and high-performing peer tutors across campus, Abednego Lomazah compiles and organizes essential resources into accessible Google Drive repositories and Studocu study packs. Our goal is to eliminate academic barriers and ensure every student has the tools needed to succeed.

Proactive Study Tips from Your University Doctor

1. Start Early with PASSCO: Do not wait until mid-semester evaluations to review past exam trends. Cross-reference lecture slides with historical question patterns early in the semester.
2. Leverage Peer Tutorials: Join our interactive revision sessions where complex formulas and conceptual frameworks are broken down into digestible, practical steps.
3. Understand Your Grading Metrics: Use our comprehensive CGPA and FGPA calculation guides to set clear targets for every academic year and track your progress accurately.
